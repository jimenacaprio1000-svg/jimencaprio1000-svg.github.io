      Work process

      How I work.

      Every project follows the same four steps, so you always know what comes next.

      1. We align on the problem, the audience and what success looks like.

      2. I audit what exists and find the friction in flows, hierarchy and content.

      3. Wireframes first, then high-fidelity UI in Figma, in light and dark.

      4. A clickable prototype to test, then clean specs ready for development.
